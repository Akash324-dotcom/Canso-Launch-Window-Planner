## How the three objections of revision 3 were resolved

1. **Visibility had no data.** Forecast side: the NCEP GEFS ensemble, the only open ensemble with visibility,
   used as a second ensemble. Observed side: the hourly observation at the ECCC station Port Hawkesbury. The row
   is evaluated everywhere and no response lacks it.
2. **The archive mixed in GFS fields under an ERA5 label.** Every model field is now ERA5: surface fields from the
   Open-Meteo ERA5 archive, CAPE from the Copernicus Climate Data Store (the keyless NSF NCAR copy was compared
   over a month and is identical). The only non-ERA5 field is visibility, which reanalysis does not have; it is
   a real observation. The archive holds no missing value: 1,137 unreported station hours of 41,568 (912 of them
   in 2022 to 2025) are filled from a named source and listed one by one.
3. **Limits were assumed.** No limit is assumed any more. The wind direction sector is computed from the site
   centre printed in the Canso environmental assessment and the gazetteer coordinates of the communities it
   names. The lightning limit is the NOAA Storm Prediction Center class boundary. The two cloud limits follow from
   the published definitions of a ceiling and of broken cloud. The evaluation window is the one the assessment
   states. Two configuration tolerances were replaced by rules that use no number of ours.

## Known-unfinished

1. **The hindcast does not verify the operational product.** No open archive keeps past runs of the ECMWF or
   GEFS ensembles in a readable form. The hindcast verifies four GFS deterministic runs per day through the same
   criteria and the same evaluation function. It shows that the event is predictable to about five days and that
   the chain is aligned; it does not show the skill or calibration of the 82-member probability.
2. **The hindcast period is under the 12 months the spec asks for.** The forecast archive starts on 2 April 2026.
   The sample has no winter. A longer period needs an archive that does not exist in the open today, or time.
3. **Spec III.4 criterion 2 is not met.** The mean calibration gap is 0.157 against a bound of 0.15. It is
   reported as a miss and no recalibration was applied; `HINDCAST.md` gives the reason. Criteria 1 and 3 are met.
   Whether gate G2 counts as passed is the gate owner's call: the contract's wording of G2 (BSS above zero,
   reliability diagram produced) is met, the spec's calibration number is not.
4. **No confidence interval is given for the skill scores.** Each lead has 164 to 173 cases and consecutive days
   are not independent. The crossover between lead 5 (BSS 0.152) and lead 6 (BSS -0.118) rests on those samples.
5. **The FORECAST boundary is now 5 days, not the 10 of spec II.7.** Issue #7 V6 prescribes this for the result
   that was found. Dates 6 to 10 days ahead are answered from climatology and labelled so.
6. **Upper-level wind is not a criterion, although the issue lists it.** This is the one place where two owner
   instructions cannot both hold. The issue asks for rows at 850, 700, 500, 300 and 200 hPa. No numeric
   upper-level wind limit is published by any operator: the real criterion is a vehicle load analysis. A row
   would need a number of our own, which the owner ruled out. The rows return in a new criteria version as soon
   as a launch provider supplies a limit; the Copernicus route can supply ERA5 pressure-level winds for them.
7. **Cold temperature is not a criterion.** The only published cold limit is the Space Shuttle's table, a function
   of temperature, wind and humidity for a cryogenic external tank. Taking one value from it would be a choice of
   our own. The hot limit of the same document is a plain threshold and is kept; it never binds at Canso.
8. **The forecast probability is a lower bound on foggy days.** Two separate ensembles cannot give the joint
   behaviour of cloud and visibility inside one member. The combination rule can understate the launch
   probability; it cannot overstate it. Measured on the archive, visibility is the only violated row on 1.6
   percent of days.
9. **Visibility is observed 48.3 km from the site.** No closer station reports it.
10. **The lightning proxy almost never binds.** CAPE at or above the "moderate instability" class did not occur in
   any evaluation window of the archive. Triggered-lightning conditions in layered cloud are covered only in
   part, by the thick cloud proxy.
11. **The wind direction row stands in for unpublished plume criteria** and ignores wind speed. Its sector is
   computed, not chosen, but the real criteria may differ.
12. **The ECCC models are not read directly.** GeoMet and the Datamart were probed and recorded. The Datamart
   serves GRIB2, which needs a decoder that `pyproject.toml` does not carry.
13. **Hour-resolved forecast probability is not exposed.** The frozen signature takes a date only.
14. **Two documents could not be read** on 3 October 2026: the ECMWF definition of the cloud layers (page not
    rendered) and the Cyclone-4M user's guide (HTTP 404, no archived copy). Both are marked in the criteria table.
15. **Nothing is pushed.** The work is committed locally on `feature-weather`. The contract asks for branch names
    of the form `weather/...`; the branch name was the owner's choice and was kept. Pushing and the pull request
    are left to the owner.
16. **Gust source.** The archive gust is the ERA5 gust as served by Open-Meteo. Over April 2024 it is 0.4 m/s
    lower on average than the Copernicus hourly maximum gust (44 against 49 hours above the 33 kt limit).

## Notes for other workflows

- API: `pyproject.toml` installs the JSON and CSV data only through an editable install (`pip install -e .`),
  which is what the contract specifies. A regular wheel would need package data declared for
  `backend/weather/data/`. Error mapping is in `README.md`. To run without the network set
  `LAUNCHWIN_WEATHER_OFFLINE=1`. `probability()` needs no key.
- API and FRONTEND: `components` has 11 rows per response, with the ids listed in `README.md`. `ensemble_size` is
  82 in FORECAST mode (51 plus 31 members). FORECAST now covers leads 0 to 5 days; later dates are CLIMATOLOGY.
- API: `from backend.weather import hindcast`; `hindcast(period_start, period_end, lead_max=10)` returns the
  spec IV.4 body and needs no network and no key. The period the committed data supports is 2026-04-02 to
  2026-09-27; `backend.weather.hindcast.longest_period("canso")` returns it. A period or lead without cases
  raises `WeatherDataError`; a lead is never padded. `backend/fixtures/skill.json` is the same body.
- FRONTEND: in the skill fixture `skill_horizon_measured_days` is 5 and four `bss` values are negative. The chart
  must show them as they are.
- `tests/contract/test_weather_schema.py` sits in a directory API owns. It is WEATHER's obligation under
  contract Seam 2 and changes no schema.
- Process: the issue asks for one subagent per task. The work was done inline by one agent. The other rules of
  the anti-corner-cutting law were followed, and `progress.md` records each red and green run.
